# Feature dictionary

Human one-liners from family module docstrings and target `META`.
Generated by `analysis.targets.build_targets`. Features use only events
with date ≤ period end. Snapshot extract fields are flagged as such.
`d_interco_share` is all-NaN: invoice/tx IDs are `COUNTERPARTY_*`,
companies are `COMP_*`, and the two sets do not overlap.

#### `b_neg_liq_3`

#### `b_min_liq_3`

#### `b_mean_liq_3`

#### `b_below_0`

- **family:** b
- **source tables:** balances, transactions, banking_products

#### `b_below_half_runway`

- **family:** b
- **source tables:** balances, transactions, banking_products

#### `b_bal_vol`

- **family:** b
- **source tables:** balances, transactions, banking_products

#### `b_neg_episodes`

- **family:** b

- **family:** e
- **source tables:** invoices
- **formula:** amount-weighted (paid_dt - due) days on invoices paid in the trailing 3 months (90 days on a weekly grid), clipped [-30, 120]. Null on the first 6 calendar months of the panel (2024-09..2025-02): left truncation hides pre-sample invoices paid late, which shortens early delays.

#### `e_delay_paid`

- **family:** e
- **source tables:** invoices
- **formula:** amount-weighted (paid_dt - due) days on invoices paid in the trailing 3 months (90 days on a weekly grid), clipped [-30, 120]. Null on the first 6 calendar months of the panel (2024-09..2025-02): left truncation hides pre-sample invoices paid late, which shortens early delays.

#### `e_credit_note_ratio`

#### `e_fx_share`

#### `e_ar_issued`

## Targets (Y)


### Module `analysis.targets.y1_forecast`

Literature: Yao, Levy-Chapira, Margaryan 2017 (arXiv 1707.00757): checking-account activity beats ratios for corporate default. Y1 is the cash-path forecast target (cross-horizon, not a same-column label).

#### `y1_net_h1`

- **family:** y1_forecast
- **source tables:** transactions, balances, banking_products
- **formula:** y1_net_h1 = operational net (op_in - op_out) at t+1
- **forbidden X:** none (cross-horizon / cross-source)

#### `y1_net_h3`

- **family:** y1_forecast
- **source tables:** transactions, balances, banking_products
- **formula:** y1_net_h3 = operational net (op_in - op_out) at t+3
- **forbidden X:** none (cross-horizon / cross-source)

#### `y1_in_h1`

- **family:** y1_forecast
- **source tables:** transactions, balances, banking_products
- **formula:** y1_in_h1 = operational inflow at t+1
- **forbidden X:** none (cross-horizon / cross-source)

#### `y1_in_h3`

- **family:** y1_forecast
- **source tables:** transactions, balances, banking_products
- **formula:** y1_in_h3 = operational inflow at t+3
- **forbidden X:** none (cross-horizon / cross-source)

#### `y1_liq_h1`

- **family:** y1_forecast
- **source tables:** transactions, balances, banking_products
- **formula:** y1_liq_h1 = reconstructed month-end liquidity at t+1 (same unwind as score_pipeline._liquidity). None of these are known at t.
- **forbidden X:** none (cross-horizon / cross-source)

#### `y1_liq_h3`

- **family:** y1_forecast
- **source tables:** transactions, balances, banking_products
- **formula:** y1_liq_h3 = reconstructed month-end liquidity at t+3 (same unwind as score_pipeline._liquidity). None of these are known at t.
- **forbidden X:** none (cross-horizon / cross-source)

#### `y2_runway_lt1_sust`

### Module `analysis.targets.y3_recovery`

Literature: Challenge brief 45->65 recovery example; FinRegLab 2025 liquidity path; Banque de France Bulletin 227/8 (only AP late >30 days moves PD). Models for y3_recover_6m must not use family E (overdue is in the label).

#### `y3_recover_6m`

- **family:** y3_recovery
- **source tables:** transactions, balances, banking_products, invoices
- **formula:** cash recover AND each of those 3 months has open-AP overdue>30d share <= 0.5 (no open AP counts as not high)
- **forbidden X:** b, e

#### `y3_recover_cash_6m`

- **family:** y3_recovery
- **source tables:** transactions, balances, banking_products, invoices
- **formula:** 1 if stressed at t and some 3 consecutive months in t+1..t+6 have runway >= 3
- **forbidden X:** b


### Module `analysis.targets.y4_debt`

Literature: Yao, Levy-Chapira, Margaryan 2017 (arXiv 1707.00757): credit-line violations and cash inflows are central for corporate default. Catalogue Y4: debt-service / inflow rise (sustained or doubling) and new facility after a cash dip (distress borrowing).

#### `y4_ds_r_gt05_sust`

- **family:** y4_debt_pressure
- **source tables:** transactions, debt_products
- **formula:** 1 if ds_r = debt_service_3m / max(in3, 1) > 0.5 in each of the next 3 months (t+1, t+2, t+3). debt_service is CAT_MAP debt_repayment only (fee / interest_charge are fin_cost).
- **forbidden X:** f

#### `y4_ds_r_double`

- **family:** y4_debt_pressure
- **source tables:** transactions, debt_products
- **formula:** 1 if ds_r at t+3 >= 2 * ds_r at t, both defined, and ds_r_t > 0.05 (floor so a near-zero base cannot explode). NaN when the floor or either side is missing.
- **forbidden X:** f

#### `y4_new_facility_after_dip`

- **family:** y4_debt_pressure
- **source tables:** transactions, debt_products
- **formula:** 1 if a debt_products.created_at falls in (t, t+6m] AND net < 0 in at least 2 of the last 3 months at t. Dip uses operational net, not reconstructed liquidity (avoids copying family B).
- **forbidden X:** f

#### `y4_ogtg_appear`

- **family:** y4_debt_pressure
- **source tables:** transactions, debt_products
- **formula:** Snapshot-only: 1 if the company has any debt_products row with outstanding_gt_granted at extract; populated only for 2026-08, NaN on every other month. Not an onset in 2024–2025.
- **forbidden X:** f


### Module `analysis.targets.y5_payment`

Literature: Hirshleifer et al. 2019 PastDue%; Banque de France Bulletin 227/8 >30 days late

#### `y5_ap_od30_ownp80`

- **family:** y5_payment
- **source tables:** invoices
- **formula:** AP share of open amount that is >30 days past due exceeds that company's own-history p80 for 3 consecutive future months. p80 uses only that company's months <= t (never future, never pooled with other companies, so holdout never enters a train percentile).
- **forbidden X:** e

#### `y5_ar_od30_sust`

- **family:** y5_payment
- **source tables:** invoices
- **formula:** AR overdue>30d share is high for 3 consecutive future months. "High" = above that company's own-history p80 (same rule as AP). A pooled train-only expanding p80 was tried first; calendar-correct 3-month persistence sat at ~2.8% (below the 5% floor), so own-history is the allowed fallback. Holdout never enters a pooled percentile.
- **forbidden X:** e

#### `y5_ap_delay_up15`

- **family:** y5_payment
- **source tables:** invoices
- **formula:** amount-weighted AP payment delay is >15 days above the trailing 6-month mean (baseline at t) in each of the next 3 months.
- **forbidden X:** e

### Module `analysis.targets.y7_concentration`

Literature: Perez-Salazar, Marquez, Vidal-Silva 2026 (Computers 15:135): supplier HHI and operational volatility beat revenue level for micro-enterprise solvency. Y7 is the customer-side analogue: loss of the current top-1 AR counterparty, with a sustained inflow drop so the event is not a silent ERP gap.

#### `y7_top1_lost`

- **family:** y7_concentration_shock
- **source tables:** invoices, transactions
- **formula:** the AR counterparty with the largest share of |amount| in months t-2..t has 0 AR issuance in t+1..t+3 (share drops to 0).
- **forbidden X:** d

#### `y7_top1_lost_inflow`

- **family:** y7_concentration_shock
- **source tables:** invoices, transactions
- **formula:** that event AND op_in in each of t+1, t+2, t+3 is more than 25% below the trailing-3m mean op_in at t (sustained; not a one-month crash).
- **forbidden X:** d


### Module `analysis.targets.y8_cross`

### Module `analysis.targets.y9_fees`

Literature: FinRegLab 2025, Sharpening the Focus: NSF counts and fee/interest pressure on bank statements predict SMB default (young firms especially). Y9 is sustained fee+interest / inflow, not a bankruptcy label.

#### `y9_fee_r_ownp80`

- **family:** y9_fee_pressure
- **source tables:** transactions
- **formula:** fee_r = (fee+interest)_3m / max(in3, 1) exceeds that company's own-history p80 for 3 consecutive future months. p80 uses only that company's months <= t (never future, never a pooled percentile, so holdout never enters a train cut).
- **forbidden X:** f

#### `y9_fee_spike`

- **family:** y9_fee_pressure
- **source tables:** transactions
- **formula:** fee_r at least doubles vs the trailing-6m mean of fee_r at t, in 2 of the next 3 months. Doubling is undefined when the trailing mean is 0 or missing (NaN, not 0) — a zero base cannot double.
- **forbidden X:** f
