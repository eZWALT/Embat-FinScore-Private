# ASCII orb slow spin

- Author: Cursor Grok
- Timestamp: 2026-09-19 19:30 CEST

## What changed

The `/orb` sphere keeps world-space lighting (so the 3D form does not wobble). A noise grain is mapped onto the surface and sampled after a slow Y-axis rotation so the glyphs crawl right to left. One turn is 32s. `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` stays static.

## Decisions

A smooth Lambert ball would look identical while spinning. Rotation has to live in the characters.
