# 2026-09-19-1815 — the going-dark / fading guard glides instead of capping at once (schema 1.3.0, scorecard v1.1)

- **Author:** Claude Code (Sonnet 5) for Javier Boix
- **Why:** sudden drops in the score. The hard cap (30 dark, 50 fading) turned a gap in the data, or a real stop, into a one-month step. Measured before the change: 706 guard onsets, median step -20.5 points (p90 -39.5, worst -67); 514 of the 638 month-on-month falls of 15+ points were the cap switching on, and 390 of those had a raw score that moved less than 5. A third of the dark spells (56 of 172) and a quarter of the fading ones (155 of 648) lasted one month and reverted (V-shapes).

## What changed

- `spec.GUARD_STEP = 10`; `scorecard.apply_guard`: while a guard is on, the ceiling is `max(cap, previous score - 10)` (the cap itself when there is no previous score) and lifts at once when the guard ends. `score = min(raw, ceiling)`. 10 is the size of the largest ordinary one-month fall (guard-free months: p5 -7, p1 -14). Not tuned on outcomes.
- New `guard_ceiling` column, `MonthRecord.guard_ceiling` and `spec.guard.max_drop_per_month` in the bundle (additive, schema 1.3.0, `scorecard_version` v1.1). The guard sentence says whether the guard is not binding yet, is on its way to the cap, or is at it. The "held at the cap" change reason only shows when the ceiling is really pinned.
- Validator checks: guard set iff ceiling set, score <= ceiling, ceiling >= cap, ceiling not more than 10 below the previous score. `guard_test.py` now asserts the glide (and that the median company is at the cap in month 6 of silence).
- Re-run: validation (train only), monitor `fit`, `evaluate`, `forecast`, full and sample bundles, `~/Desktop/Embat-handoff/bundle`. Docs updated (METHOD.md, DATA_CONTRACT.md, score README, monitor README, inspector Method tab, and the one guard sentence in `product/web/src/lib/agent/prompts/product_context.md`).

## Effect (full data)

- Month-on-month falls of 20+ points 467 -> 48; 30+ 185 -> 2; worst -67 -> -34 (what is left is the unguarded score moving); rises of 15+ 401 -> 286. Guard-onset step: mean -21 -> -9. COMP_0541: 98 -> 50 became 98 -> 88 -> 78 -> 68.
- Validation unchanged in substance: level AUROC 0.484-0.546 (was 0.480-0.545), every interval contains 0.5; sensitivity Spearman >= 0.976, AUROC move <= 0.014. The holdout section of `validation.md` is kept from the earlier (hard-cap) run and labelled; **the holdout was not looked at again**.
- Alerts: 2,374 (was 2,365); score-fall alerts still no lift (followed 29% vs 31% chance, lift 0.67-1.13); top customer unchanged (56% vs 29% base). Monitor floors moved slightly (score 3.87 -> 3.93).
- Forecast: still `reversion_quantile`, but the gain is smaller: +4.4% (1 mo) to +13.1% (6 mo), was +3.3% to +15.9%; time-split +2.2% to +8.7%. The lag-1 autocorrelation of monthly changes went from -0.14 to +0.01: the short-lag "mean reversion" was partly the cap's V-shape. Reversion at lags 3-6 (-0.10 to -0.13) remains.

## Trade-offs to keep in mind

- A persistent silence takes up to 6 months to reach 30 from a score of 85; a company whose inflows collapse reads 10 points less per month instead of all at once. Urgency is carried by the `going_dark` alert (first month, always `act`), not by the score. The first dark month can read up to about 80.
- A dropped bank connection still looks like a company that stops; the guard cannot tell them apart, it only limits how hard it punishes.
- Not addressed: the smaller jumps when a category enters or leaves the score (mostly payment history appearing/disappearing at the start or end of a series; 17% of those months move 8+ points vs 6% otherwise, guard-free). Options if wanted: phase the category weight in over a few months, or carry the last value forward for a limited time.
