## Results (train companies, holdout untouched)

- **Score-fall alerts carry no information about the eight accepted outcomes**: followed within 6 months 29% vs 31% for an alert on a random month, lift 0.72-1.24 per outcome (fees and payables below 1). Same as the step-2 validation. Median lead time 2 months where followed. The bundle ships these numbers in `alerts.json.stats` and the contract says to quote them.
- **Top customer quiet is the one alert with lift**: onset-only 56% (CI 48-63%) vs 29% base, 22% of rows flagged, recall 42%. By severity (out-of-fold ranking): top decile 62%, billed all of last 3 months 33% (near base, so `info`), rest 59%. Top-1 share is not a usable severity filter.
- 0.55 risk + 0.20 improvement alerts per company-year at fixed thresholds (materiality 8 points, chosen a priori, sensitivity in `evaluation.md`). 47% of one-month falls of 8+ points become an alert within 3 months.
- Group funnel: 2.4% of train group-months outside 3 sigma (heavy tails), 0.3% persistently; groups under 3 scored companies get no limits.
- **Forecast: a tie.** Smoothed level worse at 1-2 months, +0.4 to +1.0% at 4-6. Ship rule (must win at every horizon) not met, so `naive_last` ships with a fan that covers 50/80% out-of-fold. First thing to cut.

## Decisions

- Materiality thresholds (8/10/8 points) and severity cut-offs (12/20 points) fixed in advance, not tuned on outcomes.
- Alerts are onsets only; a going-dark alert suppresses score/category alerts for the same run; a category drop is not emitted when a score alert covers the window.
- Alert reasons for score alerts are the items that moved most since the baseline month (3 months back), with the EUR of the current window; group alerts have none and name the members instead.
- `Alert.owner`/`action` are now non-null in the contract (a 1.0.0 consumer never saw alerts, so nothing broke).
